## About Me

I help teams scale their infrastructure without downtime or spiraling costs.

- Architecting scalable **MERN** and **Next.js** environments on **AWS, Azure & GCP**
- Building **CI/CD pipelines** that let teams deploy with confidence, multiple times a day
- **Infrastructure as Code** with Terraform & Ansible, so setup is repeatable and error-free
- Container orchestration with **Docker & Kubernetes** for high-availability apps
- **Cloud migration & cost optimization** for legacy and existing systems
- Security hardening and real-time monitoring that catches issues early
